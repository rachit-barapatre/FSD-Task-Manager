Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find the answer you're looking for? Reach out to our customer support team.

Is there a free trial for the Professional plan?

Yes, we offer a fully featured 14-day free trial for the Professional plan. No credit card required until the trial ends.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time from your billing dashboard. You will continue to have access until the end of your billing cycle.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits?

We do! We offer a 50% discount for registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organizations. Please contact our support team with your tax-exempt documentation.
